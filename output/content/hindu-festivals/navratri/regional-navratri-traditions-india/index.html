Wave 5

Regional Navratri Traditions Across India

Explore 10 Navratri guides in Wave 5: regional navratri traditions across india.

Article 41

Navratri in Gujarat: The Epicenter of Garba

The scale of the celebrations, traditional attire, and the nightly rituals surrounding the Garbha Deep.

Article 42

Durga Puja in West Bengal: Pandal Hopping and Rituals

The arrival of the Goddess, artistic pandal themes, Sindoor Khela, and the emotional Visarjan on Dashami.

Article 43

Mysore Dasara: The Royal Celebration of Karnataka

The illuminated Mysore Palace, the Jumboo Savari (elephant procession), and the celebration of Chamundeshwari.

Article 44

Navratri in North India: Ram Leela and Jagratas

The nightly theatrical performances of the Ramayana leading up to the burning of Ravana effigies on Dussehra.

Article 45

Bathukamma in Telangana: The Festival of Flowers

The floral stacking rituals, songs sung by women, and the regional celebration of the life-giver Goddess.

Article 46

Kullu Dussehra in Himachal Pradesh: A Valley in Celebration

The gathering of local deities in the Dhalpur maidan, occurring slightly after the main Navratri days.

Article 47

Golu in Tamil Nadu: The Display of Dolls

The tradition of arranging wooden dolls on odd-numbered tiers, inviting guests, and singing devotional songs.

Article 48

Vidyarambham in Kerala: Initiating Learning During Navratri

The final three days dedicated to Saraswati, placing books at the altar, and ceremonies for children writing their first letters.

Article 49

Navratri in Maharashtra: Ghatasthapana and Bhondla

The installation of the pot, exchanging of marigold garlands, and the Bhondla folk dances.

Article 50

Exploring the Common Threads Across Regional Celebrations

Synthesizing the shared themes of female power, harvest, and the triumph of good over evil despite different customs.
