Editorial principles for this library

Regional precision: a custom is named as regional or household-specific when it is not universal.

Calendar accuracy: annual tithi, muhurta, sunrise and moonrise values are treated as location/year-sensitive.

Practical safety: fasting, fire, food, crowds, live animals and public events include modern safety context.

Respectful participation: simple home observance and diaspora adaptation are treated as valid rather than “less authentic.”
