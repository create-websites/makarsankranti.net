Festival Guide

"Diwali: A Luminous Journey through the Festival of Lights"

"Diwali, or Deepavali, is a significant five-day Hindu festival celebrated worldwide, symbolizing the triumph of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance. The festival honors the return of Lord Rama, his wife Sita, and devotee Hanuman to Ayodhya after 14 years of exile, invoking the spirit of 'Ram Rajya', an ideal state of governance marked by peace, prosperity, and righteousness."
