Why Durga Puja matters

A festival where worship becomes a citywide work of art

Durga Puja combines sacred customs, temporary structures, idol crafting, music, cuisine, attire, and community involvement. In Bengal, the Goddess is revered as a powerful cosmic entity and a beloved daughter coming back to her roots.

This hub categorizes 20 guides into four groups, allowing readers to explore ritual significance, crafting, community involvement, and practical Pujo experiences.
