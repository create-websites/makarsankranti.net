The Role of Panchang Shravan on Gudi Padwa

Many families kick off the New Year by consulting the panchang to understand the significance of tithi, nakshatra, yoga, and yearly forecasts in their daily lives.

Gudi Padwa Hub
Why this guide matters

The festival is made unforgettable by participating in various actions such as raising the Gudi, listening to the panchang, sharing a ritual meal, creating rangoli, and engaging children. These instructions emphasize both significance and practicality.

Beginning the year by listening to time

Panchang Shravan focuses on listening to or studying the panchang, the traditional Hindu calendar. This practice on Gudi Padwa marks the start of the New Year with families listening to key events of the upcoming year and marking auspicious dates or religious ceremonies.

Throughout history, the panchang has served both practical and ritual purposes, aiding in the coordination of festivals, fasts, ceremonies, and seasonal timings.

The five limbs of a panchang

Panchang encompasses five calendrical elements: tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga, and karana, forming the foundation of a full almanac that includes additional information.

Understanding the basics of astrology, such as the significance of tithis and lunar days, is a valuable first step, even if you don't become an expert in technical astrology.

What is heard on Gudi Padwa

In different regions and cultural traditions, a priest, elder, astrologer, or wise family member may interpret the year's name, planetary alignments, festival dates, seasonal details, and overall predictions.

Families are encouraged to practice at a level that is personally meaningful, whether it be through devotional listening or detailed astrological interpretation.

A modern household version

If nobody in the household can understand a traditional panchang, opt for a reliable regional almanac that provides essential details such as the New Year tithi, significant festivals, relevant ekadashi dates, family traditions, and temple occasions.

Develop a family calendar to share after learning about Panchang Shravan, turning it from a passive tradition into a practical tool for yearly organization.

Reflection matters as much as prediction

The true essence of the practice lies not in predicting every event, but in taking a moment at the start of a year to acknowledge the structure of time, its seasons, and sacred milestones.

Match the panchang with a brief family contemplation: What should we carry forward? What should we release? What festivals or family events do we prioritize this year?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

What is Panchang Shravan?

At the beginning of the year, it is customary to hear or review the new almanac and important calendar details.

Do I need to understand astrology to participate?

You can concentrate on the calendar, tithis, festivals, and family observances without delving into intricate astrological interpretations.

What are the five parts of a panchang?

Traditionally they are tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga and karana.
