Men’s Gudi Padwa Fashion: Kurta and Pheta

Achieve a stylish Maharashtrian festive attire by pairing a kurta with pajama or dhoti, a Nehru jacket, and the classic pheta while staying comfortable.

Gudi Padwa Hub
Why this guide matters

Clothing and home decorations transform Gudi Padwa into a festive extravaganza. These tips emphasize simple yet stylish styling and decor that create a celebratory atmosphere without being overly complicated to achieve.

Start with the occasion

When planning your attire for a home puja, neighborhood procession, cultural program, and formal community event, consider the duration and level of activity for each occasion.

A well-fitting, breathable kurta can often appear more elegant than a heavily embroidered garment that quickly becomes uncomfortable.

Kurta colors for the festival

The colors of saffron, cream, white, green, maroon, and gold blend seamlessly with Gudi Padwa's vibrant aesthetic. Solid hues complement rangoli, marigolds, and the colorful Gudi in photographs.

If the pheta is the focal point, opt for a simpler kurta to ensure a harmonious outfit.

Pajama, churidar or dhoti

Most wearers find a straight pajama or churidar comfortable. However, a dhoti-style bottom can give a more traditional appearance, but it requires secure tying and practice before a lengthy event.

Pre-stitched dhoti pants provide a convenient option for younger participants and diaspora gatherings.

The pheta as the focal point

The pheta, a traditional Maharashtrian turban, has the ability to elevate a simple kurta into a unique festive ensemble. Saffron is a popular choice for public events and processions, but various colors and regional tying methods are also utilized.

If you are unsure how to tie one, seek help from a skilled family member or opt for a pre-tied version to achieve a secure and proper fit.

Add a jacket only when needed

A Nehru jacket can add definition to a simple kurta and is perfect for evening occasions or formal pictures. But in warm weather, an extra layer might not be needed.

Comfort is key when it comes to dressing for festive occasions. Opt for breathable materials and comfortable shoes that are suitable for walking.

Coordinate without becoming identical

Instead of perfectly matching colors, families can opt for a coordinated palette when taking photographs: think saffron and cream, green and gold, or maroon and beige.

This maintains a cohesive look while allowing each individual to express their personal style.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

What is a pheta?

A pheta is a Maharashtrian headgear worn for special events, celebrations, and cultural gatherings.

Does a Gudi Padwa outfit require a dhoti?

A festive look can be achieved with a kurta paired with pajama, churidar, trousers, or the traditional option of a dhoti.

Can I use a pre-tied pheta?

Yes, it can be practical if you lack someone with experience in tying the traditional style.
