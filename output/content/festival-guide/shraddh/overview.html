Why Shraddh matters

A ritual language for memory, duty and gratitude

Shraddh provides Hindu families with a structured method to honor ancestors through rituals such as offering water, food, charity, prayer, and sharing family memories. Pitru Paksha establishes a specific lunar period solely for this purpose.
