A Plant-Based Easter Brunch Everyone Can Enjoy

Create a bountiful vegan Easter spread featuring savory entrees, vibrant spring accompaniments, and desserts that rival traditional options.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🌼 EasterCulinary & Baking Trends

Lead with real dishes, not replacements

You don't have to replicate traditional egg-and-dairy dishes to create a delicious plant-based brunch. Focus on mushroom tarts, roasted asparagus, herbed potatoes, grain salads, fruit, and breads that stand out on their own.

Use a centerpiece people can slice and share

Experiment with a vegetable galette, lentil-mushroom loaf, savory strata with plant-based custard, or a roasted cauliflower centerpiece with lemon and herbs to elevate your dining experience. Sharing a delicious main dish can make any meal feel like a celebration.

Make baking easier with a few reliable swaps

Plant-based milks, neutral oil, vegan butter, and flax or commercial egg replacers are versatile ingredients for many brunch recipes. It's best to choose recipes that are already vegan rather than trying to convert a delicate custard or soufflé at the last minute.

Label the buffet clearly

When guests have allergies in addition to dietary preferences, it is important to label dishes and keep serving utensils separate. Just because something is "plant-based" does not guarantee it is nut-free, gluten-free, or soy-free.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good vegan Easter brunch centerpiece?

A vegetable galette, mushroom-lentil loaf, or savory plant-based strata can serve as the focal point of the meal

Should I try to veganize every traditional dish?

It may not always be the case, but a menu centered on plant-based dishes typically offers a more harmonious flavor profile.
