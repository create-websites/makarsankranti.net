Why Football Became a Thanksgiving Tradition

Track the evolution of Thanksgiving football from college games in the late 1800s to its inclusion in the NFL schedule, becoming a staple in holiday traditions in Detroit and Dallas.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHistory, Heritage & Perspectives

College football helped set the pattern

Football and Thanksgiving were intertwined long before the inception of the modern NFL. In the late 19th century, college games frequently scheduled rivalry matchups on the holiday, taking advantage of the break in normal routines for students and spectators.

The connection was logical: as football and Thanksgiving grew in popularity as public traditions, newspaper coverage transformed key games into communal regional celebrations.

The NFL builds an annual tradition

Professional football has long been associated with Thanksgiving, with the NFL recognizing Thanksgiving games dating back to 1920. Detroit began hosting its first Thanksgiving game in 1934, and the Lions have continued to play on the holiday every year since 1945. Dallas became a regular Thanksgiving host in 1966.

In 2006, a Thanksgiving evening game was introduced, extending the schedule beyond the traditional Detroit and Dallas games. This has transformed the day into a complete media event for countless households.

Why the tradition stuck

Thanksgiving football is a perfect match for the day's flow, providing a shared backdrop for cooking, socializing, and unwinding post-feast. It offers a simple, inclusive activity for guests of all ages, bridging any gaps in interests.

Not all households follow the same tradition. Some may choose to skip sports altogether, while others may plan their entire afternoon around kickoff. Thanksgiving can cater to both preferences.

Make football serve the gathering: not dominate it

For guests who have different preferences, consider creating separate areas for entertainment and socializing during gatherings. This can include designated spaces for watching, playing games, or engaging in conversation, with a clear meal time for everyone to come together. Maintaining a moderate noise level and avoiding a TV at the dining table can help ensure that everyone feels included in the tradition.

Frequently asked questions

When did NFL Thanksgiving games begin?

The NFL began recording Thanksgiving games during its inaugural season in 1920.

Why are the Lions and Cowboys associated with Thanksgiving?

Detroit started hosting Thanksgiving games regularly in the 1930s and has played every year since 1945, while Dallas began its Thanksgiving hosting tradition in 1966.
