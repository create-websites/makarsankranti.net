Giving Back Locally During Thanksgiving

Show support for food banks, shelters, and delivery programs during Thanksgiving by inquiring about their specific needs and maintaining a balance between holiday generosity and consistent year-round assistance.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingGratitude, Wellness & Traditions

Ask organizations what is actually useful

Before starting a donation drive, make sure to check the specific needs, storage limitations, and volunteer requirements of food banks and shelters. It's also worth noting that cash donations can often stretch further than retail purchases, as organizations are able to buy food in bulk.

Think beyond canned-food drives

Different communities may have varying needs, such as hygiene products, transportation aid, meal delivery, winter clothing, gift cards, translation services, or ongoing financial assistance.

Volunteer with humility

Holiday volunteer positions may fill up fast. If Thanksgiving Day is already booked, try signing up for shifts before or after, or commit to a regular schedule later in the winter. Always follow staff guidance and refrain from taking photos of recipients without their consent.

Make service part of family tradition

Encouraging children to assist with organizing pantry-friendly donations, crafting supportive cards for organizations in need, or collectively selecting a cause as a family can help instill a lasting commitment to community involvement. It is important to cultivate these habits year-round, rather than solely focusing on one holiday for giving back.

Frequently asked questions

Is donating money better than donating food?

Numerous food banks can effectively maximize funds, however requirements differ. Be sure to review the organization's latest recommendations prior to making a decision.

What if Thanksgiving volunteer shifts are full?

Volunteer again or provide ongoing support. Organizations require assistance year-round, not just during holidays.
