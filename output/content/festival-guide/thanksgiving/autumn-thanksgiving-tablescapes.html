Autumn Thanksgiving Tablescapes on a Budget

Design a stylish Thanksgiving table using gourds, foliage, pinecones, candles, layered linens, name cards, and reusable decor in place of costly centerpieces.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHosting, Prep & Decor

Start with texture, not shopping

You don't need a big decor budget to create a memorable table setting. Start with your existing items like neutral plates, basic glassware, cloth napkins, and a runner. Then, enhance the autumn vibe with leaves, pinecones, branches, mini pumpkins, or seasonal fruit.

Keep the centerpiece low

Guests should have clear visibility of each other. Utilize groupings of small gourds, bud vases, low greenery, or candles instead of a single tall arrangement. When using candles, ensure that the flames are kept a safe distance from dry leaves, paper, and children.

Use napkins and place cards as color

Using a folded napkin, ribbon, or personalized name card can add a touch of elegance to your table setting without the need for additional items. Coordinating two or three colors throughout the table can elevate even the simplest decorations.

Design for food service

Decide on the placement of serving dishes before arranging the table. For family-style dinners, ensure there are ample open spaces. For buffet-style meals, feel free to add decorative elements to the dining table as the platters will be placed elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest inexpensive centerpiece?

A lineup of petite pumpkins or gourds paired with trimmed foliage and a couple of small candles is a straightforward, adaptable, and effortless way to decorate.

Should I decorate with real leaves?

You may use artificial or fresh greenery indoors for easier maintenance, but be sure to keep dry leaves away from open flames and food surfaces.
