Thanksgiving Leftovers: Gourmet Transformations

Transform your remaining turkey, stuffing, cranberry sauce, veggies, and pie into soups, sandwiches, casseroles, and fresh dishes by adhering to proper storage guidelines.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingCulinary Guides & Recipes

Cool and store leftovers first

Being creative begins with proper storage. According to USDA guidelines, it is advised to refrigerate any perishable leftovers within two hours of cooking or serving. To expedite the cooling process, divide large amounts into shallow containers.

Refrigerate Thanksgiving leftovers promptly and use within four days, or freeze any leftovers that won't be eaten within that time frame.

Turkey becomes a new protein

Incorporate sliced turkey into a pressed sandwich with cranberry sauce and a bold condiment, mix chopped turkey into enchiladas or pot pie filling, or briefly simmer it in a broth-based soup. Remember to portion out what you need to avoid constantly reheating the entire batch.

Stuffing, potatoes and vegetables

Leftover stuffing can be transformed into crispy patties topped with an egg, a tasty waffle, or used as a base for a casserole. Mashed potatoes can be turned into potato cakes or used to thicken soup. Roasted vegetables can be incorporated into grain bowls, frittatas, and pureed soups.

Desserts can become components

Break up pie for parfaits, blend pie filling into milkshakes or yogurt, or freeze for later use. This strategy minimizes waste and transforms the day after Thanksgiving into a fresh opportunity for culinary experimentation instead of simply reheating leftovers.

Note: Refrigerate perishable foods right away; if unsure about storage, throw it out instead of trying it.

Frequently asked questions

How long are Thanksgiving leftovers safe in the refrigerator?

According to USDA guidelines, refrigerated Thanksgiving leftovers can typically be safely consumed for up to four days if promptly cooled and stored properly.

Can I freeze Thanksgiving leftovers?

Certainly. Divide into meal-sized portions, date-label, and store in freezer-safe containers for optimal freshness.
