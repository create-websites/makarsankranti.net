The Evolution of the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade

Follow the evolution of the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade from a small employee parade in 1924 to a grand spectacle featuring giant balloons, performances, and a beloved television tradition.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHistory, Heritage & Perspectives

A holiday parade begins in 1924

The Macy's Thanksgiving parade originated in 1924, with employees dressed in costume parading through Manhattan alongside animals from Central Park Zoo en route to the Herald Square store. The tradition was created to provide festive entertainment and kick off the holiday shopping season.

The parade soon became a yearly tradition, evolving its look alongside the city. Live animal displays were replaced by grand showcases like floats, marching bands, celebrity appearances, and the iconic giant character balloons.

How the balloons became icons

The parade was easily identifiable from far away and on TV thanks to the oversized balloons. Designing and creating these balloons required a unique set of skills, including illustration, fabrication, inflation testing, coordination with handling teams, and careful consideration of weather conditions.

The balloons added a touch of modernity to the parade, allowing for characters to evolve with each generation while still maintaining the traditional spectacle of giant figures gliding through the streets of Manhattan.

Broadway, television and national reach

With the growth of broadcasting, the parade transformed from a local New York event into a multimedia extravaganza. Musical acts, marching bands, dance troupes, and theatrical performances were now included to entertain viewers at home and spectators lining the streets.

The parade's lasting popularity can be attributed to its dual nature as both a community event and a televised spectacle. Whether viewed from the sidewalk, living room, or online clips, families can partake in the shared holiday experience.

Planning a parade-centered Thanksgiving morning

If attending the parade is a family tradition, plan breakfast and food preparations around the broadcast instead of cooking everything at once. Keep kids' activities easy and be aware that the televised schedule and viewing locations may vary each year, so check official details before the holiday.

Frequently asked questions

When did the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade begin?

Macy’s dates the first parade to 1924.

Were giant balloons part of the very first parade?

Early parades featured live animals, while giant character balloons became a defining aspect in later years.
