A Complete Plant-Based Thanksgiving Feast

Create a delightful vegetarian or vegan Thanksgiving spread featuring main dishes, delicious sides, flavorful gravies, and decadent desserts that are festive and not an afterthought.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingCulinary Guides & Recipes

Design around a real centerpiece

For a successful plant-based Thanksgiving, choose a main dish with structure, aroma, and visual appeal. Think about options like mushroom-and-lentil Wellington, stuffed squash, savory nut roast, vegetable pot pie, or layered filo pie instead of relying solely on side dishes to make the meal complete.

Build depth with umami and browning

Utilize mushrooms, caramelized onions, miso, toasted nuts, roasted garlic, and deeply browned vegetables to infuse your dishes with the rich flavors typically found in slow-cooked meat meals. Incorporate them into gravies, stuffings, and main courses to maintain a cohesive taste profile throughout the entire meal.

Classic sides are easy to adapt

Mashed potatoes can be made using olive oil, plant butter, and unsweetened plant milk. Stuffing can be prepared with vegetable stock. Green bean casserole can be made with dairy-free mushroom sauce. Many cranberry sauce recipes are naturally plant-based.

Be sure to inspect packaged ingredients closely as some stocks, pie crusts, marshmallows, and flavorings may contain animal-derived ingredients.

Dessert should still feel like Thanksgiving

Plant-based custard ingredients can be used to make pumpkin pie, while apple crisp, fruit galettes, and pecan-style tarts are also adaptable. Serve coffee, tea, and a nonalcoholic sparkling drink to ensure the meal ends with a special touch, just like any traditional feast.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good vegan centerpiece?

Stuffed squash, mushroom-lentil Wellington, vegetable pot pie, and savory nut roasts make for hearty main dishes.

Do I need separate dishes for non-vegan guests?

A plant-based menu that is thoughtfully designed can cater to all diners. If you choose to include dairy or meat options, it's best to serve them separately instead of incorporating them into every dish.
