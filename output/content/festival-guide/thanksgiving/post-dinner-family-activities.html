Post-Dinner Thanksgiving Activities Beyond TV

Keep the Thanksgiving festivities going strong after dinner with activities such as walks, board games, memory-sharing, crafts, neighborhood events, and relaxed traditions suitable for all ages.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingGratitude, Wellness & Traditions

Take the energy outside

Taking a quick stroll can help the group digest after a big meal and allows kids to stretch their legs. In some areas, Turkey Trots are held in the morning, while in others, families opt for a post-dinner walk around the neighborhood.

Choose games with low barriers

Choose games that are easy to pick up and suitable for all ages such as charades, drawing games, trivia, cards, and cooperative board games, as they are more inclusive and enjoyable for everyone compared to complex games that may alienate casual players.

Create a family archive

Capture a grandparent recounting a family tale, go through old photos, recreate a special moment, or have everyone share a favorite memory from the year. These experiences create lasting memories that go beyond just that moment.

Leave room for quiet

Not everyone desires structured activities. Offer a puzzle table, reading nook, or craft corner for individuals to come and go as they please. A fulfilling holiday doesn't have to be filled with constant planned entertainment.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good activity for mixed ages?

Activities such as taking a stroll, playing charades, enjoying card games, sharing photos, and solving puzzles together can be enjoyed by all ages.

Should every minute be planned?

It's often best to have a few optional activities instead of sticking to a strict schedule after a big meal.
