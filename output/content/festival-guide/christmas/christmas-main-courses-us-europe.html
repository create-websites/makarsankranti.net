Christmas Main Courses: U.S. and European Traditions

Christmas main courses vary greatly across Europe and the United States due to factors such as climate, religion, migration, and regional food traditions.

Turkey and ham in the United States

Roast turkey is a popular choice for American Christmas dinners, particularly in households that continue the tradition of Thanksgiving cooking throughout December. Another popular option is baked ham, which is ideal for serving large gatherings and pairs well with pre-made side dishes.

Regional and immigrant influences can significantly alter the menu, therefore neither dish should be regarded as obligatory.

Roast goose and other European meats

In some regions of Central and Northern Europe, roast goose is closely tied to Christmas traditions, while duck, pork, and game are more common in other areas. These culinary preferences were influenced by historical availability and local farming practices well before the rise of global supermarket influences.

Modern families might opt for simpler old-fashioned recipes or select smaller cuts of meat for practical purposes.

Carp and seafood traditions

In Central and Eastern Europe, Carp is a traditional dish on Christmas Eve, while in some Catholic cultures, fish or seafood-heavy meals reflect ancient fasting customs leading up to Christmas Day.

French Réveillon menus may include oysters, shellfish, and indulgent celebratory dishes, but there is no standard national format.

Building a cross-cultural holiday menu

Select a main dish and a couple of sides that reflect a specific region's culinary tradition, rather than overwhelming the table with every popular Christmas dish at the same time. Provide explanations for unfamiliar foods so that guests can appreciate their presence on the menu.

When blending family heritages, opting for a smaller portion of two traditional main dishes can hold more significance than completely replacing one side dish.

Context note: Different regions have their own holiday food traditions. The dishes included in this guide are merely illustrations, not overarching national customs.

Frequently asked questions

Is turkey the traditional Christmas meal everywhere?

Various regions have their own traditional foods such as goose, pork, carp, seafood, and more.

Why are fish meals common on Christmas Eve?

In certain Christian traditions, they are linked to ancient practices of fasting or refraining from certain foods leading up to Christmas.
