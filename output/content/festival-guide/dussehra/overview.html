Why Vijayadashami matters

One festival, many forms of victory

Dussehra signifies various victories and celebrations, such as Rama defeating Ravana, Durga conquering Mahishasura, Vidyarambham symbolizing the return to learning, Shastra and Ayudha Puja honoring tools, and regional festivities focused on local deities and heritage.

This hub categorizes 20 guides into four groups, allowing readers to explore myth and ritual, travel, reflection, and practical celebration.
