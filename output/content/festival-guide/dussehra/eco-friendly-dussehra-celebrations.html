Eco-Friendly Dussehra: Ravan Dahan and Low-Waste Ideas

Create a greener Dussehra by opting for eco-friendly decorations, safer effigies, reusable items, organizing community clean-ups, and exploring pollution-free festivities.

Dussehra Hub
Why this guide matters

The concept of promoting good behavior over harmful urges is reinforced when the event minimizes unnecessary waste, smoke, and litter.

Start with the largest impacts

Start by addressing the main sources of waste: disposable plastic decorations, large non-recyclable effigies, litter made of various materials, food waste, and unnecessary disposable serving items.

A couple of structural adjustments hold more weight than numerous small "green" actions.

Rethink Ravan Dahan materials

When planning events, community organizers should focus on using natural or easily recyclable materials, creating smaller-scale structures that reduce the use of toxic paints, plastics, and synthetic fillers. It is important to prioritize fire-safety regulations and local laws.

In places where burning is limited or unsuitable, symbolic light, projection, theater, or reusable installations can convey the message without replicating the traditional ritual.

Decor and food with a second life

Utilize fabric torans, eco-friendly lights, minimal natural flowers, and compostable rangoli materials. Carefully portion out food and set up a secure and legal system for sharing or donating excess.

The top festive ornament is usually the one that can be stored and reused the following year.

Make clean-up part of the event

Before the celebration commences, make sure to designate waste stations, volunteers, and post-event clean-up duties. Clearly marking compost, recycling, and landfill bins is more efficient than relying on guests to figure it out on their own.

Sustainability is effective when integrated into logistics, not just tacked on as a slogan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Can Ravana Dahan be made eco-friendly?

Reducing size, using non-toxic materials, and adhering to local fire and pollution regulations can help minimize its environmental impact.

What is the easiest household change?

Replace disposable decor and serving items with reusable alternatives.

What if burning is restricted locally?

Choose a symbolic non-burning alternative and follow local regulations.
