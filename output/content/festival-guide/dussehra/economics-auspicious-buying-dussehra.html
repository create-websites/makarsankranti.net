Why People Buy Gold, Vehicles and Homes on Dussehra

Learn about the reasons behind the increase in big purchases like gold, vehicles, and property during Dussehra, and explore how factors like auspicious timing, promotions, and high demand influence consumer decision-making.

Dussehra Hub
Why this guide matters

Favorable purchasing merges cultural and economic influences, with the deadline of a significant date adding a psychological urgency to household spending decisions.

The power of an auspicious date

Vijayadashami is often linked to prosperous beginnings for numerous individuals, making it an auspicious time for purchasing a vehicle, investing in jewelry, finalizing contracts, or launching a new business endeavor.

The ritual significance eliminates doubt by transforming a business choice into a significant moment.

Promotions amplify cultural timing

During the holiday season, retailers capitalize on increased demand by offering financing deals, exchange bonuses, new product launches, and concentrated advertising efforts.

This creates a cycle: cultural intentions influence promotions, which in turn increase the commercial visibility of the event.

Pent-up demand and family decision-making

Extensive research is typically needed for significant purchases, with Dussehra serving as the selected deadline rather than the initial stage of the decision-making process.

Families can align budgets, availability, and delivery schedules to ensure the transaction occurs on a fortuitous day.

Auspicious does not mean unaffordable

It is important to maintain financial discipline when planning the festival. Consider comparing prices, financing costs, insurance, maintenance, and long-term affordability. Seek professional advice for real estate or investment decisions.

A sustainable "good start" is the best one, lasting beyond the initial celebration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Why are vehicle deliveries popular on Dussehra?

Numerous purchasers consider Vijayadashami to be a lucky day to commence owning or utilizing a new asset.

Do festive discounts always mean the best deal?

Instead of just looking at discounts, consider the overall cost, financing options, and alternative choices.

Is this financial advice?

The article delves into consumer behavior, emphasizing the importance of evaluating major financial decisions based on your individual circumstances.
