Wave 9

Uttara Kanda: Ram Rajya, Luv-Kush & Departure

Ten connected guides from the 21-Shraddh-Topics Ramayana Stories plan.

Article 81

The Uttara Kanda: The Era of Ram Rajya

Explore The Uttara Kanda: The Era of Ram Rajya: The utopian reign of Rama, where dharma, peace, and prosperity ruled without disease or crime.

Article 82

The Whispers of Ayodhya: The Washerman’s Gossip

Explore The Whispers of Ayodhya: The Washerman’s Gossip: Rumors circulating among the citizens questioning Sita's purity after her captivity in Lanka.

Article 83

The Banishment of Sita

Explore The Banishment of Sita: Rama making the heartbreaking decision as a king to exile his pregnant wife to preserve the monarchy's integrity.

Article 84

Valmiki’s Ashram and the Birth of the Twins

Explore Valmiki’s Ashram and the Birth of the Twins: Sita finding refuge with Sage Valmiki and giving birth to the princes Luv and Kush.

Article 85

The Ashvamedha Yagna (Horse Sacrifice)

Explore The Ashvamedha Yagna (Horse Sacrifice): Rama initiating the grand sacrifice using a golden statue of Sita in her absence.

Article 86

Luv and Kush Capture the Royal Horse

Explore Luv and Kush Capture the Royal Horse: The young boys defeating the armies of Ayodhya, including their uncles Lakshmana, Bharata, and Shatrughna.

Article 87

Singing the Ramayana in Rama’s Court

Explore Singing the Ramayana in Rama’s Court: Valmiki sending Luv and Kush to recite the epic, leading to Rama recognizing his sons.

Article 88

Sita’s Final Descent into the Earth

Explore Sita’s Final Descent into the Earth: Sita refusing to prove her purity a second time, calling upon her mother Bhumi to take her back.

Article 89

The Tragic Departure of Lakshmana

Explore The Tragic Departure of Lakshmana: Lakshmana breaking a vow to save Rama from Durvasa's curse, resulting in his giving up his life.

Article 90

The Jal Samadhi: The End of the Avatar

Explore The Jal Samadhi: The End of the Avatar: Rama, along with his brothers and citizens, walking into the Sarayu river to return to Vaikuntha.
