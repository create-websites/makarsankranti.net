What is numerology?

Numerology assigns symbolic meanings to numbers derived from a birth date or name. This guide explains one common Western approach; it is a reflective tradition, not a scientific measurement.

Σ

Numbers as symbols

Digits 1 through 9 are assigned broad personality and life-theme associations within the system.

1

Digit reduction

Multi-digit totals are repeatedly added until a single digit remains, subject to the chosen Master Number rule.

✦

Master Numbers

Many modern numerologists preserve 11, 22 and 33 rather than reducing them immediately to 2, 4 and 6.

Worked method

How to calculate a Life Path number

Write the full birth date, reduce the three date components separately, then combine them. Decide how you will treat Master Numbers before beginning.

Write the date

Separate the calendar month, day and four-digit birth year.

Reduce each part

Add the digits of the month, day and year down to their allowed results.

Add the subtotals

Combine the reduced month, day and year values.

Reduce the total

Continue until 1–9 remains, unless your method preserves a final 11, 22 or 33.

Worked example: June 15, 1988
Month: 6 → 6Day: 15 → 1 + 5 = 6Year: 1988 → 26 → 8Total: 6 + 6 + 8 = 20 → 2

Using the slide’s method, the result is Life Path 2.

Why two calculators may disagree Some methods preserve 11, 22 and 33 at intermediate stages; others preserve them only as the final result. State the convention and apply it consistently.

Often preserved

Master Numbers 11, 22 and 33

These are commonly read as intensified versions of 2, 4 and 6, combining added potential with added pressure. Not every numerology school uses the same rule.

11

The Intuitive

A heightened version of 2, associated with intuition and inspiration alongside sensitivity or nervous energy.

22

The Master Builder

Combines the vision linked with 11 and the practicality of 4: turning a broad idea into lasting, concrete form.

33

The Master Teacher

An elevated version of 6, associated with service and uplifting others while remembering personal needs and boundaries.

Use with perspective Numerology is a symbolic interpretive tradition, not a scientifically validated personality test or forecasting method. Enjoy it as reflection or entertainment, and do not use it as a substitute for medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.
