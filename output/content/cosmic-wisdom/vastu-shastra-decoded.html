Foundations

What Vastu Shastra describes

Vastu Shastra is a traditional Indian body of architectural and spatial knowledge concerned with site selection, orientation, proportion, layout and interior arrangement.

ॐ

A textual tradition

Sanskrit architectural traditions describe buildings, settlements, temples, orientation, form and construction.

Practical synthesis

Four recurring principles

The source emphasizes a few broad ideas more than any single room placement.

1

Align the entrance

The traditional preference is an entrance toward north, east or northeast when site constraints permit.

2

Balance the elements

Organize light, air, water, heat, mass and open space so one quality does not overwhelm the whole home.

3

Keep the center open

The Brahmasthan is traditionally treated as light, uncluttered and visually connected.

4

Follow the slope

The traditional recommendation places the higher side southwest and the lower side northeast.

i

Cultural and building-safety context

Vastu guidance is a traditional cultural framework, not a substitute for building codes, accessibility requirements, fire safety, climate-responsive design or professional engineering. Do not remove walls, columns, stairs, plumbing, wiring or ventilation systems to follow a diagram. Consult qualified architects, engineers and local authorities before construction or structural changes.
