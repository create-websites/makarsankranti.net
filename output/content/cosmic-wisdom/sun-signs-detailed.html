What a sun sign represents

Your sun sign is the familiar zodiac placement determined by the Sun's position on your birth date. In astrology it symbolizes core identity and vitality, but it is only one part of a full birth chart.

☉

Core identity

Traditionally connected with central character, ego and the way personal vitality is expressed.

◐

Set by birth date

It comes from the zodiac section occupied by the Sun at the time of birth.

✦

One piece of a whole

Astrologers also consider the Moon, rising sign, planets, houses and aspects for a fuller chart.

The organizing pattern

Four elements and three modalities

The zodiac combines one of four elements with one of three modalities, giving each sign a distinct symbolic style.

Elements

Fire

Aries, Leo and Sagittarius emphasize action, confidence and inspiration.

Earth

Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn emphasize practicality, stability and results.

Air

Gemini, Libra and Aquarius emphasize ideas, communication and social connection.

Water

Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces emphasize feeling, intuition and emotional depth.

Modalities

Cardinal

Aries, Cancer, Libra and Capricorn are traditionally associated with initiation.

Fixed

Taurus, Leo, Scorpio and Aquarius are traditionally associated with persistence.

Mutable

Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius and Pisces are traditionally associated with adaptation.

i

Interpretive context

Astrology is a symbolic and cultural tradition, not a scientifically validated method for measuring personality or predicting events. Sun-sign descriptions are broad themes for reflection or entertainment and should not be used to stereotype people or make medical, legal, financial or other consequential decisions.
