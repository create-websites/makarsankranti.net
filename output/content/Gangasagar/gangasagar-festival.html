Gangasagar Mela Festival

Festival Overview

What is Gangasagar Mela?

Gangasagar Mela is held annually on Sagar Island in West Bengal around Makar Sankranti. Pilgrims travel to the confluence of the Ganga and the Bay of Bengal for the sacred bath, prayers at Kapil Muni Temple and a collective celebration of faith. For a few days, the island becomes a vast temporary pilgrimage city serving millions of visitors.

Faith & Mythology

Why Ganga Sagar is sacred

The festival expresses ideas of purification, renewal and liberation through a holy dip at the confluence. Its sacred narrative is linked with King Sagara, his sons, Bhagiratha, the descent of the Ganga and sage Kapil Muni. In the festival tradition, the pilgrimage connects the river's sacred descent with prayer, remembrance and spiritual cleansing.

“Sab tirath bar bar, Ganga Sagar ek bar.”

A traditional saying emphasizing the special place of Gangasagar pilgrimage.

Traditions & Rituals

The traditional Gangasagar experience

The pilgrimage follows a recognizable rhythm, from arrival and camp settlement to the sacred bath and temple visit.

🌅

Dawn Holy Dip

Devotees enter the water around Makar Sankranti, often at dawn, for the sacred snan.

🛕

Kapil Muni Darshan

After the dip, pilgrims offer prayers at the temple associated with sage Kapil Muni.

🔥

Aarti & Devotion

Prayer, chanting, lamps and shared devotion shape the spiritual atmosphere of the Mela.

⛺

Temporary Pilgrim City

Camps, shelters, services and marketplaces support the huge temporary gathering.

Pilgrim Journey

How the journey unfolds

Reaching the island is itself part of the Gangasagar experience. Pilgrims move through a multi-modal network of trains, buses, ferry crossings and island transport before reaching the Mela grounds.

1

Reach Kolkata / South Bengal transit points

Rail and road services carry pilgrims toward Kakdwip, Namkhana and the mainland ferry approaches.

2

Cross the Muriganga

Ferries and vessels carry pilgrims from the mainland side toward Kachuberia on Sagar Island.

3

Travel to the Mela grounds

From Kachuberia, pilgrims continue across the island to the main congregation area and camps.

4

Snan and Darshan

The journey culminates in the holy dip followed by prayer at Kapil Muni Temple.

Safety & Infrastructure

Frequently Asked Questions

Gangasagar Mela FAQs

Where is Gangasagar Mela held?

It is held on Sagar Island in West Bengal, at the confluence associated with the Ganga and the Bay of Bengal.

When is Gangasagar Mela celebrated?

The central observance is associated with Makar Sankranti in January, when pilgrims gather for the holy dip.

What are the main Gangasagar rituals?

The principal practices described in these guides are the sacred snan at the confluence, prayer and darshan at Kapil Muni Temple, and devotional observances such as aarti and chanting.

Why is Kapil Muni important to Gangasagar?

The festival's sacred narrative connects sage Kapil Muni with the story of King Sagara, Bhagiratha and the descent of the Ganga.
