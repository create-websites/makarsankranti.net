Why dates can differ

Pitru Paksha follows lunar tithis, not a fixed Gregorian date. The civil date can shift with the city, sunrise, tithi boundaries and the rule used for the observance.

Purnima vs. Pitru Paksha

Purnima Shraddha is commonly listed with the annual Shraddha calendar. The main Pitru Paksha sequence is shown separately here from Pratipada through Amavasya.

Before fixing a ritual time

  • Confirm the city where the observance will occur.
  • Use the ancestor's death tithi when known.
  • Check a location-specific Panchang for timings.
  • Follow family or sampradaya practice where applicable.
